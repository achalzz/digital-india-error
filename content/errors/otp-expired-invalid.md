# OTP Expired or Invalid OTP Entered — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Occurs when the submitted OTP has exceeded its short server validity window, or when an earlier OTP from multiple requests was entered instead of the newest one.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Aadhaar, PAN
- **Stage of Application**: Aadhaar e-KYC or Income tax e-filing verification
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Multiple OTPs generated in rapid succession; user entered the older SMS instead of the most recent one**: Multiple OTPs generated in rapid succession; user entered the older SMS instead of the most recent one
- **Local device clock drift**: Local device clock drift: Computer or smartphone time differs from Indian Standard Time (IST)
- **SMS was delayed by telecom provider and arrived after the 5-minute validity window**: SMS was delayed by telecom provider and arrived after the 5-minute validity window

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Check your device settings: Ensure 'Date & Time' is set to 'Automatic (Network-provided time)' and timezone is GMT+5:30.
- [ ] Check the timestamp of the received SMS.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Clear all old OTP SMS messages from the government sender to prevent confusion.

### Step 2: Action 2
Synchronize your computer/phone clock with network time.

### Step 3: Action 3
Click 'Request New OTP' once, wait patiently for the SMS, and enter the latest OTP received.

### Step 4: Action 4
Ensure you type digits directly without leading or trailing spaces.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Entering 3 consecutive invalid OTPs will freeze the transaction session for 30 to 60 minutes.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Do not copy-paste OTPs with formatting symbols or characters.
