# Application Under Process Meaning & Realistic Timeline — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Indicates that your application has cleared initial computer validation and is currently undergoing internal statutory scrutiny, background checks, or physical dispatch queue.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Passport, PAN, Driving Licence
- **Stage of Application**: Post-submission processing stage
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Passport**: Passport: Application pending verification report from District Police Headquarters
- **Driving Licence**: Driving Licence: Waiting for RTO Motor Vehicle Inspector (MVI) approval after driving test
- **PAN**: PAN: Demographic matching with NSDL backend verification team
- **High regional application backlogs during festival or peak vacation seasons**: High regional application backlogs during festival or peak vacation seasons

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Read the specific sub-status in the portal tracking tab (e.g. 'Police Verification Report Submitted', 'Pending Printing').

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Check official turnaround benchmarks: Passport normal processing is typically 15–20 days; Tatkaal is 3–5 days; PAN card is 10–15 days.

### Step 2: Action 2
For Passport police verification: Ensure you are available at your residence and keep original documents ready when the local police station contacts you.

### Step 3: Action 3
If processing exceeds the Citizen's Charter timeline by more than 30 days, register a grievance via CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in).

### Step 4: Action 4
Avoid visiting regional offices in person without a scheduled inquiry appointment.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: 'Under process' is a normal operational state and does NOT mean your application is rejected or lost.
