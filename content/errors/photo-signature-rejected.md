# Photograph or Signature Rejected by Portal — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Occurs when your passport photo or signature image fails strict automated pixel dimension, aspect ratio, or background color algorithms.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Passport, Driving Licence
- **Stage of Application**: Biometric / Photo upload stage
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Photo has colored or patterned background instead of plain white or off-white**: Photo has colored or patterned background instead of plain white or off-white
- **Face covers less than 70-80% of the photograph area**: Face covers less than 70-80% of the photograph area
- **Signature scanned in low contrast or in blue ink when portal demands black ink on white paper**: Signature scanned in low contrast or in blue ink when portal demands black ink on white paper
- **Pixel dimensions outside mandated limits (e.g., Parivahan requires 35mm x 45mm, ~420x540 pixels)**: Pixel dimensions outside mandated limits (e.g., Parivahan requires 35mm x 45mm, ~420x540 pixels)

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Verify background is pure white without wall textures or shadows.
- [ ] Ensure signature is sharp, centered, and signed on blank white printer paper (not ruled notebook paper).

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Crop the photograph to 35mm x 45mm with your face occupying 75% of the frame.

### Step 2: Action 2
Sign clearly using a black gel or ballpoint pen on plain white paper and crop tightly.

### Step 3: Action 3
Use image editing software to resize image dimensions strictly to the portal's stated pixels (e.g., width: 200px, height: 260px).

### Step 4: Action 4
Save strictly as .JPEG or .JPG under 50 KB.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Selfies or casual outdoor photographs are rejected immediately during administrative scrutiny.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Avoid using beauty filters, flash reflections on glasses, or red-eye artifacts.
