## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Triggered when the mobile number you currently hold is different from the number originally linked to your Aadhaar/Passport profile during enrolment, preventing one-time password verification.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Aadhaar, Passport
- **Stage of Application**: SMS OTP login or profile verification
- **Recorded Review Date**: 2026-10-06
- **Source Status**: Needs source review; claim-level references are not recorded in the project data.

---

## Possible Causes
- **Old phone number deactivated by telecom provider due to inactivity**: Old phone number deactivated by telecom provider due to inactivity
- **Family member's mobile number was used during initial enrollment years ago**: Family member's mobile number was used during initial enrollment years ago
- **Typo in the 10-digit number entry field**: Typo in the 10-digit number entry field

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Use UIDAI's 'Verify Mobile/Email' tool at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in to check which number is on file without viewing full private numbers.
- [ ] Confirm your SIM is active and can receive National SMS headers.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Mobile number update in Aadhaar CANNOT be done purely online; biometric verification is mandatory.

### Step 2: Action 2
Visit your nearest Post Office Aadhaar Center or Bank Branch with your physical Aadhaar card.

### Step 3: Action 3
Complete biometric authentication and pay the nominal ₹50 UIDAI fee.

### Step 4: Action 4
Receive the URN (Update Request Number) and track status. Processing normally takes 24 to 72 hours.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Never share OTPs with operators claiming to update your mobile number over WhatsApp or phone calls. Mobile linking always requires physical biometrics.

- ❌ Do not repeatedly guess mobile numbers on portals to prevent temporary account lockout.
