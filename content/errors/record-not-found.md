# Record Not Found on Government Portal — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Indicates that the entered application number, licence number, or acknowledgement does not exist in the centralized national database, often due to legacy manual paper records or state database synchronization lags.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Driving Licence, PAN
- **Stage of Application**: Status check, DL renewal, or PAN verification
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Old driving licence (issued before Sarathi computerization) was never backlogged by the local RTO**: Old driving licence (issued before Sarathi computerization) was never backlogged by the local RTO
- **Application submitted in a different state or RTO jurisdiction than the one selected on the login page**: Application submitted in a different state or RTO jurisdiction than the one selected on the login page
- **Typo in the state code or series format (e.g. 'DL-04' vs 'DL04')**: Typo in the state code or series format (e.g. 'DL-04' vs 'DL04')
- **Application was only submitted within the last 2 hours and batch database indexing has not run**: Application was only submitted within the last 2 hours and batch database indexing has not run

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Verify the exact spacing format on your physical driving licence card.
- [ ] Confirm you selected the correct State on the Parivahan home screen before searching.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
For old Driving Licences: Apply for 'Backlog Entry' at your local RTO or through the Parivahan portal under 'Services on DL'.

### Step 2: Action 2
Check your receipt / SMS for the exact Application Number and Date of Birth.

### Step 3: Action 3
Wait 24 hours if the application was submitted on a weekend or public holiday.

### Step 4: Action 4
If the issue persists, visit your issuing RTO with the original paper booklet/card to verify digitization.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Do not apply for a fresh Learner's Licence if you already possess a valid old licence, as this creates duplicate driving records prohibited under the Motor Vehicles Act.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Do not consult touts outside RTO offices offering to 'pull up' lost records without official file tracking.

---
