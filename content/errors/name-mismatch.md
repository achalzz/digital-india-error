# Name Mismatch in Online Application — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Occurs when your submitted name string does not character-for-character match the backend database of the verifying authority (e.g. UIDAI database vs Income Tax CBDT record), often due to initials, expanded middle names, or maiden names.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport
- **Stage of Application**: Aadhaar e-KYC verification, PAN-Aadhaar linking, or Passport demographic check
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Initials used on one document (e.g., 'K. Sharma') while expanded on another ('Kumar Sharma')**: Initials used on one document (e.g., 'K. Sharma') while expanded on another ('Kumar Sharma')
- **Transposed first and last names in portal forms**: Transposed first and last names in portal forms
- **Maiden name retained after marriage without updating official gazette or marriage certificate**: Maiden name retained after marriage without updating official gazette or marriage certificate
- **Honorific prefixes (Mr./Ms./Dr.) inadvertently entered into the primary name field**: Honorific prefixes (Mr./Ms./Dr.) inadvertently entered into the primary name field

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Check spelling on your physical Aadhaar card and PAN card letter-by-letter.
- [ ] Verify whether middle name was omitted in one portal.
- [ ] Confirm no trailing space was added at the end of the text input box.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Determine the 'Master Anchor' document: Usually, your Aadhaar must reflect the exact spelling on your Class 10 Certificate or Passport.

### Step 2: Action 2
If Aadhaar has the error, initiate a demographic update online at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in using a valid POI (Proof of Identity) document.

### Step 3: Action 3
If PAN has the error, apply for 'Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data' via Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL.

### Step 4: Action 4
Wait for the corrected document to generate before re-attempting the linking or application submission.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Do not create a new PAN or second Aadhaar. Having more than one PAN is illegal under Section 272B of the Income Tax Act with penalties up to ₹10,000.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Never pay third-party agents claiming to bypass biometric or database name checks.
- ❌ Do not repeatedly submit mismatched applications, which can trigger system flags.
