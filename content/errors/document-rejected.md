## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Occurs when an uploaded document fails automated OCR scrutiny or manual verification due to blurriness, missing pages, wrong document category, or lack of self-attestation.

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Check portal status dashboard for specific remarks from the verification officer.
- [ ] Inspect the uploaded PDF on a desktop screen to verify all corners and text are legible.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Login to the respective portal (e.g. Parivahan or Passport Seva) and navigate to 'Upload Pending Documents'.

### Step 2: Action 2
Scan the original colored physical document at 200 to 300 DPI in PDF format.

### Step 3: Action 3
If self-attestation is required, sign across the photocopy, write 'Self Attested', and scan.

### Step 4: Action 4
Ensure file size is within limits (typically 20 KB - 500 KB) and re-upload.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Uploading falsified, tampered, or expired utility bills (older than 3 months) can lead to permanent rejection or legal scrutiny.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Avoid using mobile phone camera snapshots with table shadows or cropped corners.
