## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Appears when you attempt to create a new application while an existing active application with the same demographic details or phone number is already pending in the portal's system.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Driving Licence, Passport
- **Stage of Application**: Initial form submission or appointment booking
- **Recorded Review Date**: 2026-10-06
- **Source Status**: Needs source review; claim-level references are not recorded in the project data.

---

## Possible Causes
- **A previous incomplete application was initiated and not cancelled**: A previous incomplete application was initiated and not cancelled
- **Browser timed out previously, but the backend successfully generated an Application Reference Number (ARN)**: Browser timed out previously, but the backend successfully generated an Application Reference Number (ARN)
- **Applying for a duplicate document when the original application is still active**: Applying for a duplicate document when the original application is still active

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Check your registered email and SMS inbox for an earlier ARN or Application Number.
- [ ] Login to the portal dashboard and look at the 'Submitted Applications' tab.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Log into Passport Seva or Sarathi and click on 'View Saved/Submitted Applications'.

### Step 2: Action 2
Locate the older ARN and select 'Continue Application' or 'Pay and Schedule Appointment'.

### Step 3: Action 3
If you genuinely made mistakes in the older application, use the official 'Cancel Application' button or wait until the appointment slot lapses.

### Step 4: Action 4
Never fill out a brand-new application form until the older one is either processed or cancelled.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Creating duplicate passport applications with differing information can lead to show-cause notices from the Regional Passport Office (RPO).

- ❌ Do not create multiple accounts with different email IDs to bypass application limits.
