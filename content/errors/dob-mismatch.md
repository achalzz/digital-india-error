# Date of Birth Mismatch in Online Application — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Indicates that the Date of Birth (DOB) entered does not match the digitized date on the verifying database, or only the birth year (e.g., 01/01/YYYY) is recorded on older legacy records.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence
- **Stage of Application**: e-KYC validation or licence renewal authentication
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Legacy Aadhaar cards issued prior to 2017 often only contain the birth year, causing DD/MM mismatches**: Legacy Aadhaar cards issued prior to 2017 often only contain the birth year, causing DD/MM mismatches
- **Different date recorded on school matriculation certificate vs municipal birth certificate**: Different date recorded on school matriculation certificate vs municipal birth certificate
- **US date format (MM/DD/YYYY) entered instead of standard Indian format (DD/MM/YYYY)**: US date format (MM/DD/YYYY) entered instead of standard Indian format (DD/MM/YYYY)

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Inspect your e-Aadhaar PDF to see if DOB says 'Declared' or 'Verified'.
- [ ] Check date format required by portal input mask.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Gather a mandatory birth proof: Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Registrar or Class 10/Matriculation Certificate.

### Step 2: Action 2
Book an appointment at an authorized Aadhaar Seva Kendra for biometric DOB correction (UIDAI limits online DOB updates).

### Step 3: Action 3
Once updated, download the fresh e-Aadhaar with digital signature.

### Step 4: Action 4
Retry the portal application after 48 hours of UIDAI database synchronization.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: UIDAI permits Date of Birth correction only ONCE in a citizen's lifetime. Further changes require exception handling at a UIDAI Regional Office.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Do not submit notarized affidavits without official birth certificates, as portals routinely reject them.
- ❌ Never submit altered or digitally edited birth certificates.
