# Session Expired While Filling Online Application — Meaning, Causes & Safe Resolution

> **Editorial Standard Reminder**:
> - Never request personal identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, application numbers, bank info).
> - Cite official portals and check dates.
> - Clearly separate general network/browser troubleshooting from portal-specific guidance.

---

## Quick Answer
> **In Short**: Triggered by built-in government security timers when a form page is kept idle for more than 10 to 15 minutes, or when navigation occurs across multiple browser tabs simultaneously.

---

## Where It Appears
- **Service / Portal**: Passport, Aadhaar, Driving Licence, PAN
- **Stage of Application**: Lengthy multi-page application forms
- **Last Verified**: 2026-10-06

---

## Possible Causes
- **Taking longer than the allocated inactivity window (usually 10–15 mins) to type details or upload files**: Taking longer than the allocated inactivity window (usually 10–15 mins) to type details or upload files
- **Opening the same portal in multiple tabs, causing the session cookie to be overwritten**: Opening the same portal in multiple tabs, causing the session cookie to be overwritten
- **Using browser 'Back' or 'Forward' buttons instead of on-screen portal navigation buttons**: Using browser 'Back' or 'Forward' buttons instead of on-screen portal navigation buttons
- **Unstable internet connection that drops packet transmission intermittently**: Unstable internet connection that drops packet transmission intermittently

---

## What to Check Before Retrying (Safe Checks)
- [ ] Ensure all document scans and text details are prepared offline before starting the form.

---

## Step-by-Step Resolution

### Step 1: Action 1
Close all open tabs related to the portal and clear browser cookies for that domain.

### Step 2: Action 2
Prepare all details (addresses, parents' names, ID numbers) in a local text editor first.

### Step 3: Action 3
Log back in, click 'Save as Draft' after completing every individual step or tab.

### Step 4: Action 4
Only use the portal's internal 'Next' and 'Previous' buttons rather than the browser navigation buttons.


> [!WARNING]
> **Important Caution**: Once a session expires, unsaved form fields are wiped from server memory for citizen data protection reasons.


---

## Actions to Avoid
- ❌ Never fill lengthy government application forms over public unsecured Wi-Fi connections.
