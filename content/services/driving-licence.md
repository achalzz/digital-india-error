# Parivahan Sarathi — Public Portal Error Directory

- **Statutory Authority**: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH)
- **Official Helpline listed in project data**: 0120-2459169
- **Official Portal URL**: [https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)

## Overview
Resolves Learner's Licence (LL), Driving Licence (DL) renewal, vehicle fitness, and legacy paper-licence digitization errors across State RTOs.

## Common Citizen Issues & Blockers
- Record not found in National Register
- LL test slot booking failed
- Payment debited but receipt not generated
- Slot expired or pending scrutiny
